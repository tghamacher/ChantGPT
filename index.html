2 · Pages

Arrow keys or Page Up and Page Down turn the pages; + and − zoom.

3 · Staves

Let ChantGPT find the staves, or drag a box around a staff, from its first note to its last, to add it yourself. If its lines are off, click the staff and drag them into place.

  • Drag a staff to move it.
  • Drag the dot at the end of a line to move that end up or down.
  • Drag the bar at either end of the staff to lengthen, shorten or tilt it.
  • Lines on a page that wasn't flat bend: each bend has its own bar and dots. Double-click a staff to bend it there; double-click a bend's bar to straighten it again.

Hold H to hide the lines and see the page under them.

With a staff selected: arrow keys nudge it (Shift for more), Tab goes to the next staff, F finds its lines again, E spaces them evenly, Delete removes it, Esc lets go of it.

4 · Chant

Paste the chant in GABC (from GregoBase, say), select the staff it begins on, and place it. ChantGPT draws it on the staff in square notation, over the page, so you can see where it differs.

A chant without line breaks (as GregoBase gives it) is spread over all its staves, down to where the next chant begins, with a clef at each line's start and a custos at its end, as printed. GABC with line breaks, (z), puts each line on the next staff.

From the book's index

For a book GregoBase has indexed, such as the 1908 Graduale Romanum: open its index (made by training/gregobase.py book), say which page of the book this is, and its chants are placed page by page.

    • Drag a neume up or down to move it by steps (its GABC changes with it), or along the staff to line it up with the page.
    • Shift-drag to move just the note under the pointer.
    • With a neume selected: arrow keys move it, Enter accepts it and goes on to the next, Tab skips to the next, Delete takes it out, Esc lets go of it.
    • Click the words under a neume to correct them, or drag them into place.
    • If a line breaks in a different place from the page, select the syllable it should break at and move it to the next line (]) or the line before ([).
    • When a staff looks right, accept it (Shift+Enter): its unchecked neumes and words are checked with it. Doubtful ones, in orange, must be accepted one by one first.

    5 · Checks

    Things that are often misreadings: a custos that doesn't point to the next note, a staff without its clef, leaps of a seventh or more, flats off B, and, when the chant's mode is in its GABC headers, its final and range. Click one to go to it.

      About

      ChantGPT will read Gregorian chant from scans of printed books and manuscripts, give you the music in GABC (the format of Gregorio), and point out the places it isn't sure of so you can check them against the page. Later it will read Renaissance mensural notation too.

      This is an early version. For now you can open a scan, mark its staves, place a chant's GABC on them to compare it with the page, and correct its neumes by dragging them. Recognition comes later.
